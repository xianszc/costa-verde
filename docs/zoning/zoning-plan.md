## Where each district sits — and why

- **CBD core (x −40..40, z −35..20).** The centre of the map, not across the
  bay. 34 towers on a tight 10-unit grid plus 8 podium blocks: the tallest
  (Azzura Tower, 60 floors) dead centre, heights falling off toward the
  edges. Podiums and plazas between the towers; the elevated road and the
  monorail run east–west through the core. Glass, Art Deco crowns, rooftop
  helipads. This is the skyline the player sees from every direction.
- **Commercial ring (x −80..80, z −70..35, around the CBD).** Mid-rise
  6–15 floors, shop fronts at street level, hotels, offices, markets,
  parking garages, billboards, neon signs. Building fronts continuous along
  the street — no gaps, no empty lots.
- **Promenade + casino (x 10..80, z 20..35).** The waterfront strip of the
  commercial ring, between the marina and the CBD: Casino Azzura + Grand
  Hotel Azzura + the deco row, facing the bay. The casino forecourt and the
  casino entrance are SPLIT in the new layout (they share one point today) —
  the forecourt on the promenade walkway, the entrance at the street frontage.
- **Fortress old town (x −80..−45, z −70..−35).** A historic pocket on the
  commercial ring's south-west: narrow cobblestone streets, the fortress
  wall, the chapel, the bell tower. Same neighbourhood as today, now enclosed
  by the commercial ring instead of sitting alone at the map edge.
- **Marina + harbor (x −40..20, z 28..35).** The waterfront next to the CBD
  and the commercial ring, bay side: the quay, the harbormaster pavilion,
  moorings, the ferry lane. The harbour basin is water; the strip is land.
- **Lighthouse point (x −120..−95, z 30..55).** A headland jutting into the
  bay at its north-west edge: the lighthouse, the keeper's house, a small
  chapel. The only land in the bay.
- **Residential ring (x −115..115, z −105..35, around the commercial ring).**
  Low/mid-rise apartments and townhouses (2–6 floors), gardens, small parks,
  schools, chapels. Density falls the farther out.
- **Funicular hills (x −60..60, z −105..−75).** The slope behind the
  residential ring: terraced streets climbing the hill, retaining walls, the
  funicular line running up the middle.
- **Cliff villas (x ±95..115, z −80..−20).** The outer residential ring on
  the highest cliffs: villas with gardens, 1–3 floors, sparse.
- **Olive-grove countryside + quarry/industrial (outer edge).** The thin
  strips at the map's west/east edges and the far south: olive groves,
  farmhouses, the quarry and its sheds. Sparse but not empty — 14 buildings.

## Height falloff (mandatory) — the height-by-distance curve

Heights are a function of distance `r` from the CBD centre (0, −10), encoded
in the zoning data with small random variation so it does not look artificial:

```
floors(r) = clamp(round(60 · e^(−r/25) + 1) ± 15%, 1, 60)
```

| r (units) | 0 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 | 120 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| floors | 60 | 41 | 28 | 19 | 13 | 6 | 3 | 2 | 1 |

That gives exactly the required bands: CBD centre 40–60, CBD edge 20–40,
commercial ring 6–15, residential ring 2–6, villas/outskirts 1–3. The skyline
reads like a small mountain from every direction, because the curve is radial
— it does not depend on which side of the CBD you stand on.

## Block structure and roads

- **Tidy grid, adapted to the coast and slopes.** Avenues (thick lines on the
  diagram) at 40-unit spacing, side streets (thin lines) at 20-unit spacing.
  Clear intersections, no random roads. The grid bends only where the coast
  (the bay shoreline) and the hill slope demand it.
- **Blocks are rectangular, ~20×20 units** (some double blocks on the
  avenues). Buildings fill the block edges, 2–4 rows deep, with a courtyard
  or alley in the middle. Corner buildings are distinct (taller, or a
  different facade). No empty lots except planned parks and plazas.
- **Per block, consistent:** sidewalks, street trees, lamp posts, signage,
  crosswalks. Buildings align to the street frontage.
- **Roads connect all zones.** The main boulevard runs east–west through the
  CBD (the old boulevard, kept as the town spine). The waterfront boulevard
  runs along the shore. The ring road circles the CBD. North–south avenues
  link the hills to the harbour. You can drive from the harbour, through the
  commercial ring, into the CBD, and out to the hills and the countryside
  without leaving a road.

## Data and performance

- The city is generated from a **data-driven zoning map** (block types,
  height ranges, facade styles per zone) — the same data that drew the
  diagram. **One source of truth** for roads, blocks and buildings, shared
  with traffic, the minimap and navigation: the road data drives the mesh,
  the traffic lanes, the navigation graph and the minimap lines.
- **Instancing, merged geometry, LOD** for the tower cluster and the
  repeated facades. **Draw-call target: under ~600 in the worst view**
  (today: drive 249, walk 404, title 548 — the new city must stay in that
  band even with 517 buildings, which is why the towers and facades are
  instanced, not one draw call per building).

## What changes vs the current city

| Thing | Today | In the new plan |
|---|---|---|
| CBD / skyline | 12 towers on a landmass ACROSS the bay (z 62..104), reached by a bridge | 34 towers in the CENTER of the land (x −40..40, z −35..20); the bridge and the separate landmass go away |
| Bay | Water between the town and the skyline | The north edge of the map; the marina, promenade and lighthouse sit on it |
| Old town | South edge of the map (z −70), on its own | Historic pocket on the commercial ring's south-west (x −80..−45, z −70..−35) — same streets, now enclosed by the ring |
| Boulevard | East–west spine at z ≈ 0, south of the bay | Same spine, now running through the CBD at z ≈ −10 |
| Promenade + casino | Far east (x 60..108) | Waterfront strip of the commercial ring (x 10..80, z 20..35) |
| Residential | Barely exists (a few villas) | A full ring: apartments, townhouses, hills, cliff villas, parks, schools |
| Outer edge | Empty | Olive groves + quarry/industrial, sparse but not empty |
| Monorail + elevated road | Across the bay to the skyline | Through the CBD core, east–west |
