## Scale — the real map size in meters, and the drive time

**1 map unit = 10 m.** Land: x −120..120, z −105..35 → **2,400 × 1,400 m**
(33,600 units² plus the lighthouse headland). The map was REDUCED from the
v2 plan (3,200 × 1,850 m) per Xian's correction — a smaller, dense city
beats a large sparse one.

**Drive time corner to corner:** the diagonal is √(2400² + 1400²) ≈ **2,780 m**.
At top speed 45 m/s (162 km/h, a fast GTA-style car) that is **≈ 62 s** —
the ~60 s target. At a cruising 30 m/s it is ≈ 93 s; a cross-town drive
(harbor → CBD → hills) is ≈ 35–45 s.

## Height falloff (mandatory) — the corrected height-by-distance curve

The first plan's curve (`60·e^(−r/25) + 1`) was WRONG at the CBD edge
(~12–20 floors at r 27–40, spec 20–40). The revised curve has a **plateau**
and a **slower falloff**, with **zone clamps** that guarantee the bands:

```
r < 10:    60 floors (plateau; ±15% variation → 51–60)
10–45:     linear 60 → 15
r > 45:     14·e^(−(r−45)/30) + 1
clamps:     CBD 20–60, commercial 6–15, residential 2–6, outskirts 1–3
```

| r (units) | 0 | 15 | 30 | 45 | 60 | 80 | 100 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| floors | 60 | 54 | 34 | 15 | 9 | 5 | 3 |

Check: the CBD edge (r 27–40) is **38–21 floors** (spec 20–40 ✓); the
commercial ring (r 45–60) is 15–9 (spec 6–15 ✓); the residential ring
(r 60–100) is 9–3 (spec 2–6, clamped ✓); the outskirts (r > 100) are 3–1
(spec 1–3 ✓). The skyline reads like a small mountain from every direction,
because the curve is radial — it does not depend on which side of the CBD
you stand on.

## Where each district sits — and why (v3, stage 1)

- **CBD core (x −28..28, z −30..18).** The centre of the map, not across the
  bay. **50 towers on a tight 6-unit grid plus 8 podium blocks**: the tallest
  (Azzura Tower, 60 floors) near the centre, heights falling off toward the
  edges (radial curve). The main boulevard (12 units wide, z −6..6) runs
  east–west through the core. Podiums and plazas between the towers; the
  elevated road and the monorail run east–west through the core. Glass, Art
  Deco crowns, rooftop helipads. This is the skyline the player sees from
  every direction. Tower-block area (excl. boulevard): 2,016 units².
- **Commercial ring (x −60..60, z −54..30, around the CBD).** Mid-rise
  6–15 floors, shop fronts at street level, hotels, offices, markets,
  parking garages, billboards, neon signs. Building fronts continuous along
  the street — no gaps, no empty lots.
- **Promenade + casino (x 8..60, z 14..30).** The waterfront strip of the
  commercial ring, between the marina and the CBD: Casino Azzura + Grand
  Hotel Azzura + the deco row, facing the bay. The casino forecourt and the
  casino entrance are SPLIT in the new layout (they share one point today) —
  the forecourt on the promenade walkway, the entrance at the street frontage.
- **Fortress old town (x −60..−34, z −52..−26).** A historic pocket on the
  commercial ring's south-west: narrow cobblestone streets, the fortress
  wall, the chapel, the bell tower. Same neighbourhood as today, now enclosed
  by the commercial ring instead of sitting alone at the map edge.
- **Marina + harbor (x −30..8, z 22..30, plus the north waterfront strip
  z 30..35).** The quay, the harbormaster pavilion, **3 piers jutting into
  the bay with moored boats, boat sheds, and a harbor road**. The harbour
  basin is water; the strip is land.
- **Lighthouse point — a real headland (stage 2).** NOT a grey box in the
  water: a **peninsula joined to the land** at the map's north-west corner
  (x −120..−95, z 35..60, ~500 units²), with **cliffs on its west edge,
  the lighthouse at the tip, 2 cottages, a chapel, and a road up its spine**
  connecting it to the land. The only land in the bay.
- **Residential ring (stage 2; x −95..95, z −85..30, around the commercial
  ring).** Low/mid-rise apartments and townhouses (2–6 floors), gardens,
  small parks, schools, chapels. Density falls the farther out.
- **Funicular hills (stage 2; x −60..60, z −85..−60).** The slope behind
  the residential ring: terraced streets climbing the hill, retaining walls,
  the funicular line running up the middle.
- **Cliff villas (stage 2; x ±80..95, z −60..−15).** The outer residential
  ring on the highest cliffs: villas with gardens, 1–3 floors, sparse.
- **Olive grove + countryside, west (stage 2; x −120..−95, z −105..35 —
  25 units deep).** Olive groves, farm plots, vineyards in rows, farmhouses,
  dirt roads.
- **Quarry + industrial, east (stage 2; x 95..120, z −105..35 — 25 units
  deep).** **2 quarry pits, 3 silos, a crane, sheds — a driveable site**
  with a quarry road.
- **South countryside (stage 2; x −95..95, z −105..−85 — 20 units deep).**
  Farms, vineyards, dirt roads, farmhouses. Sparse but not empty.

## Elevation — the land rises from the bay to the hills

The land is NOT flat. It rises from the bay toward the funicular hills and
the cliff villas, so the CBD skyline shows from the water and from the hills:

| Place | Elevation |
|---|---:|
| Bay water | 0 m |
| Shore / quay / promenade | +2 m |
| Marina | +3 m |
| CBD core (city centre) | +8 m |
| Commercial ring | +10–15 m |
| Residential ring | +15–30 m |
| Funicular hills (terraced) | +30–60 m |
| Cliff villas (highest cliffs) | +50–80 m |
| South countryside (drops back) | +15–25 m |

**Terraces and retaining walls** step the hillside (3 terrace lines across
the funicular hills, 2 along the cliff villas) — the streets climb in
terraces, not in one steep slope. From the water, the CBD towers rise from
+8 m, so the full 20–60 floors read against the sky. From the hills
(+30–60 m) you look DOWN across the residential roofs at the skyline. The
diagram carries the terrace lines, the elevation labels, and a cross-section
profile strip at the bottom (bay → shore → CBD → hills).

## The waterfront — continuous promenade and quay

A **continuous promenade and quay runs along the WHOLE shore** (x −120..120,
z 30..35): a paved walkway with a quay edge, from the lighthouse headland in
the west, past the marina piers, along the promenade + casino, to the
residential waterfront in the east. No gaps, no dead ends — the player can
walk or drive the entire waterfront.

## Block structure and roads

- **Tidy grid, adapted to the coast and slopes.** Avenues (thick lines on the
  diagram) at 30-unit spacing, side streets (thin lines) at 15-unit spacing.
  Clear intersections, no random roads. The grid bends only where the coast
  (the bay shoreline) and the hill slope demand it. Dirt roads in the
  countryside and the quarry (south road at z = −95, outer roads at
  x = ±105).
- **Blocks are rectangular, ~15×15 units** in the commercial and residential
  rings (some double blocks on the avenues). Buildings fill the block edges,
  2–4 rows deep, with a courtyard or alley in the middle. Corner buildings
  are distinct (taller, or a different facade). No empty lots except planned
  parks and plazas.
- **Per block, consistent:** sidewalks, street trees, lamp posts, signage,
  crosswalks. Buildings align to the street frontage.
- **Roads connect all zones.** The main boulevard runs east–west through the
  CBD (the old boulevard, kept as the town spine). The waterfront boulevard
  runs along the shore. The ring road circles the CBD. North–south avenues
  link the hills to the harbour. You can drive from the harbour, through the
  commercial ring, into the CBD, and out to the hills and the countryside
  without leaving a road.
- **Stage 1 note:** the playable ground stays flat (y = 0) for driving; the
  elevation (bay 0 m → shore +2 → CBD +8 → hills +30–60) is expressed in
  stage 1 through the terraced hillside backdrop at the map's south edge
  and the tower heights. Stage 2 grades the terrain for real.

## Data and performance

- The city is generated from a **data-driven zoning map** (block types,
  height ranges, facade styles per zone) — the same data that drew the
  diagram. **One source of truth** for roads, blocks and buildings, shared
  with traffic, the minimap and navigation: the road data drives the mesh,
  the traffic lanes, the navigation graph and the minimap lines.
- **Instancing, merged geometry, LOD** for the tower cluster and the
  repeated facades. The draw-call estimates in the table assume: towers
  instanced by ~6 archetypes (CBD ~35), commercial facades instanced by ~8
  archetypes with merged shop fronts (~120 for the whole ring), residential
  facades instanced by ~6 archetypes (~75), and the shared row (~45) covers
  the merged road mesh, the terrain, the water, the sky, the instanced
  vegetation, traffic, peds, the player and the HUD. **Worst view ~330,
  target < 600** — the new city must stay in that band even with 651
  buildings (337 in stage 1), which is why the towers and facades are
  instanced, not one draw call per building.
